FAQs

Hajj Packages — Common Questions

What is a Hajj package?

A Hajj package is an all-in-one arrangement for the pilgrimage. HajjCall's Hajj packages cover the Hajj visa, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, tented accommodation in Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah, full-board meals, ground transport, Qurbani and an English-speaking religious guide. Airfare is booked separately so you can choose your own airline.

How much does a Hajj package cost in 2027?

HajjCall's 2027 Hajj packages range from about $10,395 to $18,495 per person. The price depends on the sharing type (Quad, Triple or Double), the trip length (10 to 20 days) and whether the Makkah stay is Shifting or Non-Shifting. Airfare is not included.

What is the difference between Shifting and Non-Shifting Hajj packages?

In a Non-Shifting package you stay in the same hotel for your entire time in Makkah. In a Shifting package your hotel changes during the trip, usually from Makkah to Aziziyah. Non-Shifting is more comfortable and costs more.

Who are these Hajj packages available to?

The Hajj packages and pricing on this page are currently available only to pilgrims travelling on a Pakistani passport. Pilgrims from other countries should request a quote for options available to them.

Is airfare included in the Hajj package price?

No. The listed prices are land packages and do not include the air ticket, so you can choose the airline and route that suits you. HajjCall can book flights separately on request.

What documents do I need for Hajj?

A passport valid for at least six months, recent white-background photos, a meningitis (ACYW135) vaccination certificate and a completed booking form. Our team handles the Hajj visa application once your documents are submitted. See the Hajj 2027 guide for the full checklist.

How do I book a Hajj package with HajjCall?

Submit the free quote form or message us on WhatsApp with your preferred package, sharing type and dates. An advisor confirms availability and pricing, you pay a reservation deposit to lock your place, then send your documents so visa processing can begin. See how booking works.
