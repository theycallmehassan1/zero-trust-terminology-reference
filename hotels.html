FAQs

Hotel Booking — Common Questions

Which countries can I book a hotel in through HajjCall?

HajjCall books hotel-only stays in Dubai (UAE), Qatar, the USA, the UK, Turkey and Malaysia — useful for stopovers, layovers and family visits around a pilgrimage. We can also arrange hotels in other countries on request.

Does HajjCall book hotels in Makkah and Madinah?

Yes. Makkah and Madinah hotels are built into every Hajj and Umrah package, with the exact property, number of nights and meal plan listed for each departure. You can also see our featured Makkah & Madinah Umrah hotel deals on this page — tap the 🕋 filter above.

How do I get a room rate?

The rates shown are indicative starting prices. Message us on WhatsApp with your city, dates and number of guests and we send live pricing and availability, usually within a few hours.

Are these hotels near the Haram?

The Dubai, Qatar, USA, UK, Turkey and Malaysia hotels are not near the Haram. Our Makkah & Madinah hotel deals — Anjum, Le Meridien Towers, Emaar Royal, Saja Madinah and Shaza Regency Plaza — are all within easy reach of Masjid al-Haram or Masjid an-Nabawi, and are the same properties featured in our Hajj and Umrah packages.
