FAQs

Hajj & Umrah Services — Common Questions

What is included in every HajjCall package?

Every HajjCall package includes Saudi visa processing, hotel accommodation in Makkah and Madinah, airport and inter-city transport, a daily breakfast (full board on Hajj), an English-speaking religious guide and a guided ziyarat tour. Flights are included unless you choose a land-only package.

Does HajjCall handle the Saudi visa?

Yes. Full Hajj and Umrah visa processing, including the Saudi e-Visa, is handled in-house for every package. You provide your passport, photos and vaccination certificate, and our team submits and tracks the application on your behalf.

Can I book flights only or hotels only?

Yes. While most pilgrims book a complete package, HajjCall also arranges hotel-only stays in Makkah and Madinah and land-only packages without flights. Tell us what you need in the quote form and we will price it separately.

Are the religious guides English-speaking?

Yes. Every group is accompanied by an experienced, English-speaking scholar or group leader who leads the rites of Hajj or Umrah, explains each step, and is available throughout the trip to answer questions.

Is travel insurance included?

Travel insurance is included in the Premium and VIP tiers and can be added to any package on request. It covers medical treatment and trip disruption during your stay in Saudi Arabia.

How do I get a price for my trip?

Submit the free quote form with your departure city, dates, group size and room preference. A travel advisor replies within 24 hours with tailored options and full pricing. No payment is needed to get a quote.
