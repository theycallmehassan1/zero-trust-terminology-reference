FAQs

Umrah Packages — Common Questions

What is an Umrah package?

An Umrah package bundles the parts of the lesser pilgrimage into one booking. HajjCall's Umrah packages include the Umrah visa, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, and transfers between Makkah, Madinah and the airport, in Double, Triple or Quad sharing. Airfare is booked separately so you can pick your own airline.

How much does an Umrah package cost?

HajjCall's fixed-departure Umrah packages start from around $965 per person for Quad sharing and go up to about $3,775 for premium hotels and longer stays. Price depends on the hotels, room-sharing type, trip length and season. Ramadhan is the most expensive period.

When can I go for Umrah?

Umrah can be performed at any time of year. HajjCall has confirmed group departures from October 2026 through August 2027, including Ramadhan 2027, and can also arrange custom dates on request.

Is airfare included in the Umrah package price?

No. The listed prices are land packages covering visa, hotels and transfers. Flights are booked separately so you can choose your airline and route. HajjCall can arrange flights on request.

How long does an Umrah trip take?

The Umrah rites themselves take three to five hours. HajjCall's Umrah packages run from about 9 to 14 nights, split between Makkah and Madinah, so pilgrims have time for extra prayers, ziyarat and rest. See the step-by-step Umrah guide.

What is included with the hotels?

Each departure lists its exact Makkah and Madinah hotels, the number of nights and the meal plan (usually breakfast). Many properties are within walking distance of the Haram. Browse our hotels for more options.

How do I book an Umrah package?

Choose a departure and tap its price to message us on WhatsApp, or submit the free quote form. An advisor confirms the hotels and price, you pay a deposit to reserve your place, then send your passport and photos so the Umrah visa can be processed. See how booking works.
