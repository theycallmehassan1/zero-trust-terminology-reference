FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a taxi?

Tap any “Book Now” button on this page. WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message to +966 50 765 1503 — just add your trip details and send.

Do you pick up from Jeddah Airport?

Yes. Share your flight number and arrival time and your driver will meet you at arrivals, including late-night flights.

Which vehicle should I choose?

Toyota Camry for up to 4 passengers, GMC Yukon XL or Hyundai Staria for families up to 7, Toyota Hiace for groups up to 12, and Toyota Coaster for larger groups up to 22.

Do you offer Ziyarat tours?

Yes — guided Ziyarat of the historic sites in Makkah and Madinah, with flexible timing for your group.

How do I pay?

The price is agreed on WhatsApp before your trip. Payment details are confirmed with your booking.
